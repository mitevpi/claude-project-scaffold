## Superpowers specs and plans

`superpowers:brainstorming` writes a design document to `superpowers/specs/`.
`superpowers:writing-plans` writes the matching implementation plan to
`superpowers/plans/`. Both get committed with the work they describe. They record
intent at a point in time. Do not polish them afterwards, and do not list them here:
the directories are the index.

- `superpowers/specs/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-design.md`
- `superpowers/plans/YYYY-MM-DD-<feature-name>.md`
